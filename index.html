    Mathematical Model of PRNG Defects:

    In vulnerable wallets, the state of the pseudorandom number generator is described by the recurrence relation:

    $$S_{t+1} = g(S_t, \theta)$$

    where \(S_t\) is the internal state of the generator, and \(\theta\) represents environmental parameters (e.g., a 32-bit timestamp \(T_{ms}\)). If the dimensionality of the initialization space \(\theta\) is limited to \(2^{32}\), then the actual entropy of the generated sequence \(H_{\text{real}}(X)\) compresses to 32 bits. The space of possible BIP-39 phrases drops from \(2^{128}\) to just \(\approx 4.29 \times 10^9\) variants.
